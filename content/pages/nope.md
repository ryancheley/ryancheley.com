Title: Nope

Things I don't do, don't use, or don't recommend — your mileage may vary.

# Tech I Don't Use

- **JavaScript frameworks** for simple sites — I'll reach for a static site generator every time
- **Cloud-native complexity** (Kubernetes, microservices) when a single VPS and Docker Compose will do
- **Overly broad permissions** — I prefer scoped, least-privilege access everywhere

# Habits I've Dropped

- **Subversion** — migrated everything to Git years ago and never looked back
- **Managing servers manually** — everything is automated or containerized now

# Not For Me

- **Comment sections** — [don't read them](https://www.ryancheley.com/dont-read-the-comments/)
- **Half marathons in the summer** — did 13 in 13 months, that was plenty

---

*This list is personal preference, not a judgment of anyone else's choices.*
